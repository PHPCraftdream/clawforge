# Code of conduct

Clawwright follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the
work rather than the people doing it. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others'
private information are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to phpcraftdream@gmail.com. Reports are handled
confidentially by the maintainer, who may remove content or restrict participation in
response.
