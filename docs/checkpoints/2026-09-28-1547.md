## Decisions

- Layout limits (7 entries per directory, 700 lines per file) are not raised. Hitting one
  means regrouping by meaning. This is recorded in CONTRIBUTING.md and in the error text
  of layout.check.ts. Rejected: raising the limits, or placing files by free slot.
- Backups: no built-in encryption. An extension point instead: AppDefinition afterBackup
  and beforeRestore, which reuse the settings/secrets hook model rather than recipe hooks.
- apply-config stays a separate command. Its --dry-run is the only real validation against
  the target, and --dump --force is the only deliberate overwrite; folding them into apply
  would complicate the plan model.
- The passthrough exemption for a misplaced --app is derived from declarations (commands
  with a variadic argument), not a hard-coded list.
- --opt=value is split into two tokens once, in runApp, so direct argv scanners agree with
  the declared parser.
